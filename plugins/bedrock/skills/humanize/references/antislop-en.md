# Anti-slop, English

Language-specific tells for EN output. Use with `antislop-core.md`. These are defaults and a
safety net: the owner's EN voice (see profile) already avoids most of this naturally, so if
you're matching that voice you'll rarely trip the list. Watch it hardest when writing fresh
or in document mode.

The point of a ban list is not to make text sound stilted. It's to catch the words AI
reaches for by reflex. Swap for the plain word, don't just delete and leave a hole.

## Banned words (swap for the ordinary equivalent)

delve, tapestry, landscape (as a metaphor for a field), realm, testament, pivotal, robust,
leverage (as a verb), utilize, harness (as a verb), streamline, seamless, vibrant, intricate,
multifaceted, myriad, plethora, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, game-changer, unlock, unleash,
elevate, embark, foster, underscore, showcase, boast, boasts, navigate (as a metaphor),
bustling, meticulous, nuanced, holistic, paradigm, synergy, ecosystem (outside real tech
use).

Swaps: utilize → use, leverage → use, delve into → look at / dig into, robust → solid /
reliable, cutting-edge → new, showcase → show, foster → build / support, myriad / plethora →
lots of / many.

## Banned phrases

"it's worth noting", "it's important to note", "in today's fast-paced world", "in today's
ever-evolving landscape", "in the realm of", "at the intersection of", "dive deep", "let's
dive in", "shed light on", "the key takeaway", "the bottom line", "in conclusion", "in
summary", "to sum up", "that being said", "needless to say", "rest assured", "when it comes
to", "a wide range of", "a testament to", "plays a crucial/pivotal role", "stands as", "serves
as a reminder".

## Banned sentence openers

Certainly, Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, Overall, Ultimately, Importantly, Notably,
Indeed, Essentially, Fundamentally. Start with the actual subject instead.

## The dashes

Zero em-dash (—) and en-dash (–). Replace with:
- a period, if the two halves can stand alone
- a comma, for a light aside
- parentheses, for a true aside
- a colon, if the second half explains the first

Wrong: "The fix is live — it just needs a deploy."
Right: "The fix is live. It just needs a deploy." or "The fix is live, it just needs a deploy."

## The trailing "-ing" analysis clause

AI tacks on a participial clause that pretends to add analysis: ", highlighting the need
for...", ", showcasing its potential", ", underscoring the importance of...". Cut it, or make
it a real separate sentence with a real point.

## Not-X-but-Y

Covered in core, but it is especially common in EN. "It is not about speed, it is about
consistency." Say the positive claim: "It is about consistency."

## Keep

The owner's real EN habits are not slop and must survive de-slopping: warm openers, leading
with the outcome, casual connectors ("Yeah", "Oh", "Btw", "good call"), a light "my bad".
Don't sand these off in the name of anti-slop. No trailing ")" though, that one is gone.
