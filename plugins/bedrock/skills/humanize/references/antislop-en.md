# Anti-slop, English

English-specific tells. Use with `antislop-core.md`. Phrases lint rejects outright are in
`stoplist.txt`. Swap for the plain word; do not just delete and leave a hole.

## Words

delve, tapestry, realm, testament, pivotal, leverage (verb), utilize, harness (verb),
streamline, seamless, vibrant, intricate, multifaceted, myriad, plethora, groundbreaking,
cutting-edge, game-changer, unlock, unleash, elevate, embark, foster, underscore,
showcase, navigate (metaphor), meticulous, holistic, paradigm, synergy.

Swaps: utilize, leverage → use; delve into → look at; showcase → show; foster → build;
myriad, plethora → lots of; cutting-edge → new.

## Phrases

"it's worth noting", "in today's fast-paced world", "in the realm of", "let's dive in",
"shed light on", "key takeaway", "in conclusion", "needless to say", "rest assured", "when
it comes to", "a wide range of", "plays a crucial role", "serves as a reminder", "I hope
this helps", "let me know if you have any questions", "don't hesitate to reach out".

## Openers

Certainly, Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, Ultimately, Importantly, Notably,
Essentially. Start with the subject instead.

## Trailing analysis clause

", highlighting the need for...", ", underscoring the importance of...". Cut it or make it
a real sentence with a real point.

## Apostrophes

Whether the user writes "don't" or "do not" is in `facts.md`. Some people never use
contractions in English; then lint rejects every apostrophe. Otherwise contractions are the
casual default.

## Dashes

When dashes are off: a period if both halves stand alone, a comma for a light aside,
parentheses for a real aside, a colon when the second half explains the first.
